§ 03 / 05

About us

UAB “VALSVA” has been working on construction projects since 1995. Our main activity is water supply, sewerage, heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems – from design to maintenance. We are a certified contractor.

We also provide services in earthworks, outdoor paving and external utility networks. We have completed projects of various sizes and significance on a wide range of construction sites, and we have long experience and strong expertise in every area we specialise in.

We work with construction companies, including as a subcontractor, and with private clients. We also offer consulting and project management services to help our clients bring their ideas to life and achieve the results they want.

We are open to cooperation with other companies and partners to create reliable and efficient solutions for managing construction projects and sites. We aim to be a reliable partner for our clients and to ensure that their projects are completed on time, meet the highest quality and safety standards, and are durable and efficient.
